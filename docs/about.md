# Yiyang Tan

<p class="page-deck">Computer science undergraduate at ShanghaiTech University. Interested in how intelligent systems reason, learn, and earn our trust.</p>

I'm a senior undergraduate in **Computer Science and Technology at ShanghaiTech University**, with an expected graduation in 2027.

My research interests are **LLMs and agents**, **benchmarking and evaluation**, and **multimodal time series forecasting**. I am particularly interested in learning from feedback and in understanding when contextual information helps a model make a better prediction.

## Research

I'm a co-author of [ARISE: Adapting to Evolving Capability Gaps in Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://mldi.group/ARISE/) and [KairosAgent: Agentic Time Series Forecasting with Fused Semantic Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30002), the latter accepted to **EMNLP 2026 Main Conference**.

For KairosAgent, my work included baseline experiments, inference workflow debugging, and results and error analysis. I am also exploring the relationship between data predictability, textual context, and model confidence in forecasting.

[See selected work →](index.html#research)

## Teaching & building

I've served as a teaching assistant for **CS100: Introduction to Programming**, helping students with programming, assignment discussions, and debugging.

My projects and coursework have included Go backend development, C++ game development, and RISC-V. I work with Python, PyTorch, C/C++, Go, Git, and Linux, and have participated in the [GeekPie](https://geekpie.club/) community.
